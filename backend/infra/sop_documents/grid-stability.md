# SOP-01: Grid Instability and Underfrequency Remediation

## 1. Overview
This operating procedure governs actions to take when the grid stability index drops below 60.0% or grid frequency falls under 49.5Hz. Underfrequency indicates demand exceeds supply.

## 2. Emergency Steps
1. **Reduce Non-Essential Load:** Shed residential and commercial sectors immediately if frequency is under 49.5Hz.
2. **Increase Generation Output:** Scale gas turbine Epsilon or hydro dam Delta outputs to restore grid balance.
3. **Preserve Critical Infrastructure:** Medical Complex D (Hospital) online status MUST remain active. Under no circumstances should the hospital sector be disabled or shed.
4. **Data Centers Load Adjustment:** Data Centers E priority 1 allocation can be reduced to a minimum of 95% of current demand during peak crises.
